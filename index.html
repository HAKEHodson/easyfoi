What information do you want?

Be specific. Include dates, places, departments, or document types if you know them. You are asking for recorded information the authority holds, not opinions or new analysis.

Tips for better requests:
  • Narrow the date range where possible
  • Name a department or team if you know it
  • Ask for data in a reusable format (CSV, spreadsheet) if relevant
  • Avoid fishing expeditions — focus on what you actually need

How FOI works

        

          

            
Search first

            
WhatDoTheyKnow publishes over a million FOI requests and responses. Searching there (and the authority’s own website or disclosure log) can save you time and avoid unnecessary requests. Use the search tools above or the button in the request wizard.

          

          

            
Your rights

            
Under the Freedom of Information Act 2000, anyone can request recorded information held by public authorities in England, Wales, Northern Ireland, and UK-wide bodies. Scottish bodies are covered by the separate Freedom of Information (Scotland) Act.

            
You do not need to say why you want it. The authority must confirm whether it holds the information and provide it (or a valid reason for withholding) within 20 working days.

          

          

            
What you can ask for

            
Emails, letters, reports, contracts, spreadsheets, meeting minutes, CCTV, voice recordings, policies, statistics — anything recorded. You cannot force them to create new information or answer questions that require analysis they don’t already hold.
